The Industry Problem

  • Outdated Methodologies: Most brands still rely on intuition, existing product lines, or sales data rather than scientific approaches to shade development.
  • Exclusionary by Design: Color gets pushed to the end of product development, shaped by constraints instead of guided by rigorous systems.
  • No Industry Standard: Labs and manufacturers lack a systematic, biology-based framework for inclusive complexion product development.
  • Cultural Disconnect: R&D teams translate vague briefs into physical shades without context on cultural nuance or regional diversity.
  • Costly Misses: Shade failures like Youthforia lead to reformulation waste, shelf delays, and brand damage—all preventable with better systems.
