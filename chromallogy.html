Software That Helps Beauty Brands Build Inclusive Complexion Products

Chromallogy is the first software built on an entirely new color space that uses biomarkers in skin to create a complete map of human skin tones. We help beauty brands, OEM/ODM labs, and in-house R&D teams develop foundations, concealers, and complexion products with systematic, science-backed methodology. For the first time, shade development has a scientific framework instead of costly trial-and-error. In an industry where one failed launch can shutter an entire brand, replacing guesswork with certainty isn't optional.
